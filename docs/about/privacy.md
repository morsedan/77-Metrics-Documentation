# Privacy

> This page summarizes how 77 Metrics handles your data. A full privacy policy will be added before the app is published.

## What 77 Metrics collects

77 Metrics collects the following data while a drive is active:

- GPS location, speed, altitude, and course
- Accelerometer data (for G-force measurement)
- Barometric pressure (for altitude calculations)

Collection begins when you tap **Start Drive** and stops when you tap **Stop**. No data is collected outside of an active drive session.

## What 77 Metrics does not collect

- Personally identifiable information
- Usage analytics or crash reports sent to a third party
- Your location outside of active drive recording

## Data storage

All drive data is stored in the app's private container on your device. It is included in iCloud backups if you have iCloud backup enabled for this app. 77 Metrics does not have access to your iCloud backup data and does not transmit your location data to any server.

## Data deletion

You can delete individual drives from trip history at any time by swiping the row or using the delete option in the drive summary. Deleting the app removes all associated data from your device.

## Location permission

77 Metrics requires location access to record drives. You can change or revoke permission at any time in **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → 77 Metrics**. Revoking location access stops new drives from recording; existing saved drives are not affected.
