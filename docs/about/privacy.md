# Privacy

!!! note
    This page will contain the 77 Metrics privacy policy. It requires legal review before publication.

## Summary

77 Metrics collects GPS location data while a drive is active. All data is stored locally on your device and is never transmitted to any server.

### What 77 Metrics collects

- GPS location, speed, altitude, and course while a drive is active
- Accelerometer data for G-force measurement while a drive is active
- Barometric pressure for altitude calculations while a drive is active

### What 77 Metrics does not collect

- Personally identifiable information
- Usage analytics or crash reports sent to a third party
- Location data outside of active drive recording

### Data storage

All drive data is stored in the app's private container on your device. It is included in iCloud backups if you have iCloud backup enabled for this app. 77 Metrics does not have access to your iCloud backup data.

### Data deletion

You can delete individual drives from trip history at any time. Deleting the app removes all associated data from your device.

### Location permission

77 Metrics requires location access to record drives. You can revoke permission at any time in iOS Settings. Revoking location access stops new drives from recording; existing saved drives are not affected.
