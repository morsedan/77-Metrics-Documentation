# About 77 Metrics

77 Metrics is a GPS vehicle computer for iPhone. It turns your iPhone into a real-time dashboard showing the metrics that matter while you drive: speed, elevation, distance, G-force, and more.

## Design principles

- **No account required.** All data stays on your device.
- **Fully offline.** GPS works without a cellular or Wi-Fi connection.
- **Customizable.** Every tile, size, and page is under your control.
- **Accurate.** Altitude uses barometric + GPS fusion. Speed and distance use GPS with accuracy filtering.
