# Trips & history

Trip history stores all your saved drives and lets you review, organize, and export them.

## Trip history list

The history list shows all saved drives in reverse chronological order. Each row shows the drive's name (or date if unnamed), distance, duration, and date.

Tap a drive to open its full summary, including route map, speed profile, elevation chart, and G-force data.

## Drive summary

The drive summary screen presents all recorded data for a drive:

- **Overview** — Distance, time, speed, and elevation statistics
- **Speed profile** — Speed over time, with moving/stopped segments highlighted
- **Elevation** — Elevation profile, gain, loss, and net change
- **G-Force** — Peak G-force and heat map of acceleration events
- **GPS** — Interactive route map
- **Vertical speed** — Climb and descent rate over time

## Naming drives

You can name a drive on the Drive Complete screen or later by tapping the drive in history and editing its name.

## Trip groups

Related drives can be grouped into a **trip** — for example, the outbound and return legs of a road trip, or multiple days of driving in a region.

To create a trip group, select two or more drives in history and tap **Group**. Give the group a name. The grouped drives remain accessible individually; the group provides a combined summary.

## Exporting drives

Drives can be exported for use in other apps or for archival. The export format includes route and timing data suitable for importing into mapping or analysis tools.

## Deleting drives

Swipe a drive in history to reveal the delete option. Deleting a drive removes it permanently. If the drive belongs to a trip group, it is also removed from the group.
