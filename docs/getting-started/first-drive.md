# Your first drive

77 Metrics is ready to use immediately after granting location permission. This page walks you through starting, pausing, and completing your first drive.

## Grant location permission

77 Metrics requires location access to track your drive. On first launch, the app prompts you to grant permission.

- **Allow While Using App** — Location is tracked when the app is in the foreground.
- **Allow Always** — Enables location updates if the app moves to the background while driving.

If you skipped the permission prompt, go to **Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → 77 Metrics** and grant access.

## Start a drive

Tap **Start Drive** on the dashboard. The button state changes to indicate an active drive, and all live metrics begin updating as GPS data arrives.

The first few seconds may show dashes (—) for some metrics. This is normal — GPS needs a moment to acquire a signal, and some metrics require several readings before they can be calculated.

## Pause and resume

Tap **Pause** to suspend data accumulation without ending the drive. The location service continues running so GPS re-locks instantly when you resume.

Tap **Resume** to continue. All accumulators — elapsed time, distance, elevation — resume from their paused values. The time spent paused does not count toward elapsed time or moving time.

## Stop and save

Tap **Stop** to end the drive. The Drive Complete screen appears with a summary of your statistics including distance, moving time, max speed, elevation gain, and a route map.

From the Drive Complete screen you can:

- Enter a name for the drive (optional)
- **Save** it to trip history
- **Discard** it without saving

## Interrupted drives

If the app closes unexpectedly during a drive, your data is not lost. 77 Metrics checkpoints drive data to disk approximately every 30 seconds. On next launch, the app detects the interrupted recording and offers to recover it so you can review and save the drive normally.
