# GPS Info

**Label:** GPS · **Short:** GPS

The GPS tile shows your current geographic position and the accuracy of the GPS fix.

## What is displayed

- **Latitude** — in decimal degrees to five decimal places (e.g., 37.33182°)
- **Longitude** — in decimal degrees to five decimal places (e.g., −122.03118°)
- **Horizontal accuracy** — the radius of uncertainty around the reported position, shown as ±n ft or ±n m

A smaller accuracy value means a more precise fix. Accuracy below ±10 m (±33 ft) is typical in open sky conditions.

## When accuracy is unavailable

If the GPS system reports that horizontal accuracy cannot be determined, the tile shows "accuracy unavailable" instead of the ±n value.

## Troubleshooting

If horizontal accuracy is large (±50 m or more), move to an area with a clearer view of the sky or wait for the GPS receiver to refine its fix. GPS accuracy improves over the first 30–60 seconds of operation.
