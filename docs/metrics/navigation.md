# Navigation metrics

Two metrics describe the direction of travel.

---

## Heading

**Label:** Heading · **Short:** HDG · **Unit:** °

Current direction of travel expressed as a compass bearing in degrees (0–360). 0° is north, 90° is east, 180° is south, 270° is west.

Heading is derived from the GPS course — the direction of motion over the ground — not from the iPhone's magnetic compass. It is therefore accurate for moving vehicles but may be unavailable or unstable at very low speeds.

With Enhanced Information enabled at Size 2 or larger, the tile adds a rotating compass rose with cardinal direction labels (N in red, E/S/W in white) alongside the bearing value.

Shows a dash (—) when GPS has not yet provided a valid course reading.

---

## Direction

**Label:** Direction · **Short:** DIR

Current direction of travel expressed as a 16-point compass label: N, NNE, NE, ENE, E, ESE, SE, SSE, S, SSW, SW, WSW, W, WNW, NW, or NNW. Each sector spans 22.5°.

Direction uses the same GPS course as Heading. At larger tile sizes with Enhanced Information enabled, the tile displays a travel-path map overlay showing recent route history alongside the compass indicator.

Shows a dash (—) when GPS has not yet provided a valid course reading.
