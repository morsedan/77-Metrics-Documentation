## Feature highlights

Three to five concise points below the hero. Each has a short title and two to three sentences of supporting copy.

---

### 1. 21 metrics. Your layout.

Choose from 21 GPS-derived metrics and arrange them on a grid of resizable tiles. Seven tile sizes let you give your most-watched readings more space. Build multiple dashboard pages to keep speed, elevation, and time data organized the way you think about your drive.

---

### 2. Elevation that stays accurate.

77 Metrics fuses your iPhone's barometric altimeter with GPS altitude to produce stable, reliable elevation readings. Track altitude above sea level, cumulative gain and loss, grade, and net elevation change — even on long drives where GPS altitude alone would drift.

---

### 3. G-force. Live and on the record.

The accelerometer tracks longitudinal and lateral forces throughout your drive. See live G-force on a dashboard tile and review a heat map of acceleration events across the full session when you stop.

---

### 4. Your data, exported your way.

Every drive exports to CSV (one GPS sample per row) or GPX 1.1 with Garmin TrackPointExtension v2 — compatible with Garmin Connect, Strava, Komoot, and any tool that reads standard GPS files. No proprietary format. No lock-in.

---

### 5. Works everywhere you do.

Configure up to 8 metrics on your vehicle's CarPlay screen. Lock Screen widgets show your current readings at a glance. A Live Activity keeps your metrics visible in the Dynamic Island throughout the drive. All without pulling out your phone.

---

## Trust indicators

Short statements that address common hesitations.

- **No account required.** Open the app and drive.
- **Works offline.** GPS does not need a cellular or Wi-Fi connection.
- **Data stays on your device.** Nothing is uploaded or transmitted to any server.
- **iOS 17 or later.** Works on all iPhone models supported by iOS 17.
