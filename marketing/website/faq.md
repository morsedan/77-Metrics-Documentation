## Questions and answers

### What is 77 Metrics?

77 Metrics is an iPhone app that shows live GPS-derived metrics on a customizable dashboard while you drive, and saves a detailed record of every session. It tracks 21 metrics — including speed, elevation, G-force, distance, direction, and grade — on a grid of resizable tiles you arrange yourself.

---

### What can it track?

Speed, average speed, moving average, max speed, vertical speed, distance, trip distance, altitude, grade, elevation gain, elevation loss, net elevation, elevation profile, elapsed time, moving time, stopped time, heading, direction, G-force, max G, and GPS coordinates with accuracy — 21 metrics in total.

### Does it need an internet connection?

No. GPS works without a cellular or Wi-Fi connection. 77 Metrics works entirely offline — you can drive into an area with no signal and every metric keeps updating normally.

---

### Does it upload my data anywhere?

No. All drive data stays on your iPhone. 77 Metrics does not transmit your location or any drive data to any server.

---

### Does it require an account?

No account is required to use any feature of the app.

---

### How is the elevation data calculated?

77 Metrics combines your iPhone's built-in barometric altimeter with GPS altitude. The barometric sensor is very sensitive to small altitude changes; GPS provides an absolute elevation anchor. Together they produce more accurate and stable elevation data than GPS alone.

---

### Does it work with CarPlay?

Yes. 77 Metrics appears in the CarPlay app launcher and shows up to 8 configurable metrics on your car's built-in display, updated every second. Drive recording is controlled from the iPhone — CarPlay is a read-only display.

---

### Does it support widgets?

Yes. 77 Metrics includes a Home Screen widget, two Lock Screen widgets (circular and rectangular), and a Live Activity that displays your metrics in the Dynamic Island and on the Lock Screen during an active drive.

---

### Can I export my drive data?

Yes. Any drive can be exported as:
- **CSV** — one row per GPS sample, suitable for spreadsheets and data analysis
- **GPX 1.1** — compatible with Garmin Connect, Strava, Komoot, and most mapping apps

---

### What iPhone and iOS version do I need?

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. CarPlay requires a compatible vehicle or aftermarket head unit. Live Activities require iOS 16.1 or later.

---

### Can I pause a drive and resume it later?

Yes. Tap Pause to suspend recording without ending the drive. GPS keeps running so the signal is ready immediately when you resume. Time spent paused is excluded from elapsed time and moving time.

---

### What happens if the app closes during a drive?

Your drive is checkpointed to disk approximately every 30 seconds. If the app closes unexpectedly, 77 Metrics detects the interrupted recording on the next launch and offers to recover it so you can review and save it normally.
