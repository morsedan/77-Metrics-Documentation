# Product positioning

## What 77 Metrics is

77 Metrics is an iPhone app that shows live GPS-derived metrics on a customizable dashboard while you drive and saves a detailed record of every session for later review.

It is a data tool, not a navigation tool. It does not provide turn-by-turn routing, traffic alerts, speed limit warnings, or maps for planning a route. It is for people who want to understand the data behind their drives.

---

## Target audience

### Primary: data-minded drivers

People who find their car's built-in instruments insufficient and want richer, more configurable live data. They already own an iPhone and are comfortable adjusting settings to get the experience they want.

### Secondary: mountain and elevation-route drivers

Drivers who regularly travel through terrain with significant elevation change — mountain passes, hill routes, switchbacks — and want accurate, real-time elevation data. The barometric + GPS fusion is a meaningful differentiator here.

### Secondary: performance driving enthusiasts

Hobbyist drivers who want G-force data alongside speed and time metrics. This audience overlaps with autocross, track day, and spirited road driving communities, though 77 Metrics does not position itself specifically toward any of these.

### Adjacent: GPS data exporters

People who want their drive data in standard formats for analysis, visualization, or logging. The CSV and GPX export paths serve this audience without requiring it.

---

## What sets 77 Metrics apart

| Differentiator | Details |
|---------------|---------|
| 21 configurable metrics | More than typical car instruments or single-purpose GPS apps |
| Barometric + GPS altitude fusion | Stable elevation readings even where GPS altitude drifts |
| 7 tile size levels | Unusually granular control over information density |
| Standard export formats | CSV and GPX 1.1 — data is not locked in |
| G-force + navigation in one app | Rare combination without a dedicated hardware unit |
| No account, offline-first | No sign-up, no server, no subscription |
| CarPlay + widgets + Live Activities | Works across iPhone, Lock Screen, Dynamic Island, and car screen |

## What 77 Metrics is not

Be explicit in copy and positioning about what the app does not do. Do not let marketing imply these features.

- **Not a navigation app** — no turn-by-turn, no routing, no traffic
- **Not a safety tool** — no collision warning, no lane departure, no driver assistance
- **Not a fleet or commercial tool** — designed for personal use; no remote tracking, no multi-vehicle management
- **Not a fitness app** — though it could be used for cycling or running, it is not positioned or optimized for those use cases
- **Not a subscription** — no ongoing cost; no premium tier implied unless that changes

---

## Key message hierarchy

Lead with these in order when space is limited:

1. **What it does:** Live GPS metrics on a customizable dashboard
2. **Unique capability:** Barometric altitude fusion for accurate elevation data
3. **Data ownership:** Everything stays on your device — no account, no server
4. **Breadth:** 21 metrics, CarPlay, widgets, export

---

## Tone

- **Direct.** State what the app does. Avoid vague language like "takes your driving to the next level."
- **Factual.** Claims should be verifiable. Avoid superlatives ("most accurate," "best in class") without evidence.
- **Practical.** The audience is technically curious. Specific details (7 size levels, GPX 1.1, barometric fusion) are assets, not noise.
- **Unpretentious.** Do not imply the app is for professional racers or experts only. It is for anyone who wants more data.

---

## Claims to avoid

- "Most accurate GPS app" — GPS accuracy is hardware-limited; this is unverifiable
- "Real-time" for widgets — widgets update on a schedule, not in true real-time
- "Perfect for [specific motorsport]" — we have not validated the app for any specific motorsport use
- Any claim about how the app performs in specific extreme conditions (desert heat, arctic cold, tunnels) beyond what the barometric drift guard handles
- Anything implying medical, safety, or regulatory approval
