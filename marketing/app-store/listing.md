## Description

**Character count target:** Under 4,000 characters. Current draft: approximately 1,800.

---

Turn your iPhone into a real-time GPS dashboard. 77 Metrics shows 21 live metrics — speed, elevation, G-force, distance, direction, grade, and more — on a fully customizable tile grid while you drive, and saves a detailed record of every session.

**21 METRICS. YOUR LAYOUT.**
Choose from 21 metrics and arrange them on a grid of resizable tiles. Seven size levels let you give important readings more visual space. Build multiple dashboard pages for different metric sets — one for elevation data, another for speed, another for time.

**ACCURATE ELEVATION DATA**
77 Metrics combines your iPhone's barometric altimeter with GPS altitude to produce stable, accurate elevation readings. Track altitude, elevation gain, elevation loss, net elevation change, and grade — all from one screen.

**G-FORCE**
The accelerometer records acceleration and braking forces throughout your drive. See real-time G-force on a dashboard tile and review a full heat map of events across the session when you stop.

**RECORD AND REVIEW**
Every drive is checkpointed automatically every 30 seconds. If the app closes unexpectedly, your recording is recovered on the next launch. After stopping, a complete summary shows your route on a map, a scrubable speed profile, an elevation chart, vertical speed history, and G-force data. Combine related drives into named trip groups for road trips and multi-day outings.

**EXPORT YOUR DATA**
Export any drive in:
• CSV — one row per GPS sample with coordinates, altitude, speed, course, and accuracy
• GPX 1.1 with Garmin TrackPointExtension v2 — compatible with Garmin Connect, Strava, Komoot, and most mapping applications

Trip groups export as a combined GPX with one track per drive.

**CARPLAY AND WIDGETS**
Configure up to 8 metrics for your vehicle's CarPlay screen. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets show your current readings at a glance. A Live Activity keeps your metrics visible on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island throughout the drive.

**NO ACCOUNT. NO SUBSCRIPTION. NO SERVER.**
All drive data stays on your iPhone. Nothing is transmitted. No sign-up is required to use any feature of the app.

Requirements: iPhone with iOS 17 or later. CarPlay requires a compatible vehicle or head unit. Live Activities require iOS 16.1 or later.
